Notes

  • Cooler, fitting: remove the old heatsink from the module, clean off the old paste (isopropyl alcohol), apply the supplied compound, mount the new unit with its holder and screws, and plug the fan into the carrier's fan header.
  • Power plug, why: the screw-terminal barrel plugs already bought loosen under vibration. A plug with its leads soldered or crimped at the factory doesn't; the right angle keeps it low against the board so vibration has less leverage on it.
  • Power plug, fit: 5.5 × 2.5 mm, centre-positive: the same size as the screw-terminal plugs it replaces and my laptop's port — take one to the shop to compare. The leads go to the existing XL4015 buck output (19.0 V), soldered and heat-shrunk or into the WAGO lever block, never a screw terminal. The jack has no lock, so the plug is still held only by friction: cable-tie the lead to the frame within ~5 cm of the jack, and optionally put a bead of silicone (RTV) across plug and jack.
