Why a buck module and a chip?

The LED strip and fan get their own 5 V supply (item 3), so a wiring fault on an arm can't take down the flight computer. The chip (item 4) makes the LED data signal strong enough for a 5 V strip.

1 · LED strip

Starling 2 Max parts

1Addressable LED strip Sim Lim TowerBuy: 1 metre

covers both drones (2 × 16 LEDs) with spares
One bare WS2812B segment: 3 pads at each cut (+5V, Din/DO, GND)
Photo: John McMaster, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0
Ask for“Do you have WS2812B addressable LED strip, 5 volt, 60 LEDs per metre, the narrow bare kind without the silicone sleeve? The newest version, V5, if you have it.”
SpecMust beWhy
TypeIndividually addressable, single data wire: WS2812B (or SK6812)The drone sends one data signal that sets each LED separately
Wires3: +5 V, data, ground4-wire strips need a clock line the drone doesn't have
Voltage5 V12 V strips won't light
LED spacing60 LEDs per metre (16.7 mm apart)4 LEDs fit on each ~75 mm arm
Width10 mm or less; 5 mm “narrow” is idealThe arms are about 10 mm wide
FinishBare (IP30), no silicone sleeve or tubeA sleeve makes it ~14.5 mm wide and heavier
ColoursRGB is fine; RGBW (SK6812) also worksSoftware handles both
Chip versionWS2812B V5 preferred, if the shop knowsAccepts the drone's 3.3 V data even on a 5 V supply
✗ Do not buy: APA102, DotStar, SK9822 or WS2801 (4 wires) · WS2815 (12 V) · Non-addressable RGB strip (whole strip one colour) · Silicone-sleeved or tube “waterproof” strip · 30 LEDs per metre (only 2 fit per arm)
✓ Check: Hold the strip against a ruler: 10 LEDs should span ~167 mm, and the width must be ≤ 10 mm.

Shape shown is 8 mm thick: buy 30 × 30 × 10 mm
Photo: Adafruit (product 3368)
Backup blower: side outlet. Must be 30 × 30, 5 V, 2 wires
Photo: The Pi Hut
Ask for“A 30 by 30 by 10 millimetre DC fan, 5 volt, two wires only. And a 30 millimetre blower fan, 5 volt, also two wires.”
SpecMust beWhy
Size30 × 30 × 10 mm (“3010”)Fits the 36 mm-wide board stack; the 10 mm-thick version pushes harder through the 2 mm gaps
Voltage5 V DCThe drone's fan port gives 5 V; a 12 V fan barely turns
WiresExactly 2: red (+) and black (−)Speed is controlled by switching the ground; 3- and 4-wire fans stutter or won't start
Current0.15 A (150 mA) or less on the labelThe fan port is limited to ~0.4 A including start-up
BearingBall or fluid / hydraulic (“FDB”)It will vibrate; plain sleeve bearings wear out
PlugAnyCut off: the fan is soldered to the buck module (item 3)
Backup blower30 × 30 × 10 mm blower (“snail shell”, side outlet), 5 V, 2 wires, ≤ 0.15 APushes harder into a single narrow gap
Reference partSunon MF30100V1-1000U-A99 (5.5 CFM, 0.12 A)Any brand matching the rows above is fine
✗ Do not buy: 12 V or 24 V fans · 3-wire (tacho) or 4-wire (PWM) fans · 25 mm or 40 mm sizes · Sleeve-bearing fans
✓ Check: Compare against your printed 30 × 30 × 10 mm reference block.

3–4 · Buck module and level shifter

Starling 2 Max parts
How these are used: the buck module turns the battery's 12–16.8 V into 5 V for the LED strip and the fan, separately from the flight computer. The 74HCT125 lifts the drone's 3.3 V LED data signal to 5 V.

35 V step-down (buck) module Sim Lim TowerBuy: 2 (+1 spare)

one per drone: powers the LED strip and the fan
MP1584EN (~22 × 17 mm)
Photo: Kunkune
Mini-360 (~17 × 11 mm)
Photo: Kunkune
Ask for“An MP1584 step-down buck converter module. A Mini-360 is also fine.”
SpecMust beWhy
Output5.0 V, 1 A or more (MP1584 ~3 A)Powers the LED strip and fan, separately from the flight computer
InputAccepts ≥ 17 V (MP1584 28 V, Mini-360 23 V max)Runs from the 4S battery, up to 16.8 V
SizeMP1584 ~22 × 17 mm; Mini-360 ~17 × 11 mmLight; mounts at an arm root
Adjustable?Trim screw: set 5.0 V with a multimeter before useOften shipped at another voltage
✗ Do not buy: Linear 7805 regulators (would burn ~6 W as heat) · Big LM2596 boards (heavy)

4Level-shifter chip Sim Lim TowerBuy: 2 (+1 spare)

Shown: a 74-series DIP-14 chip. Buy 74HCT125
Photo: Stefan Riepl, Wikimedia Commons, public domain
Ask for“74HCT125, the quad buffer chip, in DIP-14.” (The letter T matters.)
SpecMust beWhy
Part74HCT125 (or 74AHCT125)Raises the drone's 3.3 V LED data signal to 5 V for the strip
PackageDIP-14 (through-hole)Easy to hand-solder onto a scrap of perfboard
With ita 100 nF ceramic capacitor (item 6) and a small perfboard scrapStandard decoupling for logic chips; keeps the assembly tidy
✗ Do not buy: 74HC125 or 74LS125 (wrong input levels)
✓ Check: If there's no 74HCT125 anywhere: RS Singapore / element14 Singapore stock it online.
