1Addressable LED strip Sim Lim TowerBuy: 1 metre

covers both drones (2 × 16 LEDs) with spares
One bare WS2812B segment: 3 pads at each cut (+5V, Din/DO, GND)
Photo: John McMaster, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0
Ask for“Do you have WS2812B V6 addressable LED strip, the version that runs on 3.3 volts? The narrow kind with 2020 size LEDs, 60 per metre, no silicone sleeve.”
SpecMust beWhy
ChipWS2812B V6: 5050 (WS2812B-B-V6), 3535 (Mini-V6) or 2020Their datasheets support a 3.3 V supply: in spec on the drone's 3.3 V pad. Test: all LEDs off draws only µA
Wires3: +5 V, data, ground4-wire strips need a clock line the drone doesn't have
VoltageSold as a “5 V” strip; the chip must be V6WS2812C and older WS2812B (3.7 V min) and SK6812 (3.5 V) run below spec at 3.3 V; 12 V strips won't light at all
LED spacing60 LEDs per metre (16.7 mm apart)4 LEDs fit on each ~75 mm arm
Width10 mm or less; 2020 strips are ~4–5 mmThe arms are about 10 mm wide
FinishBare (IP30), no silicone sleeve or tubeA sleeve makes it ~14.5 mm wide and heavier
ColoursRGBThe RGBW strips use SK6812, not rated for 3.3 V
✗ Do not buy: WS2812C, SK6805 or SK6812 strips (not rated for 3.3 V) · APA102, DotStar, SK9822 or WS2801 (4 wires) · WS2815 (12 V) · Non-addressable RGB strip (whole strip one colour) · Silicone-sleeved or tube “waterproof” strip · 30 LEDs per metre (only 2 fit per arm)
✓ Check: Hold the strip against a ruler: 10 LEDs should span ~167 mm, and the width must be ≤ 10 mm.
Not in any Tower shop's online catalogue (checked 2026-10-25): call Sun-Light and 123 LED Lighting first. Online fallback (check the receipt suits the Concur claim): the listing must name the V6 chip. Any WS2812B or WS2812C still works on 3.3 V, below spec.

SpecMust beWhy
Size30 × 30 × 10 mm (“3010”)Fits the 36 mm-wide board stack; pushes through the 2 mm gaps in a shroud
Voltage5 V DCIt runs from the VOXL 2's fan port (J2), which gives 5 V
WiresExactly 2: red (+) and black (−)J2 switches the ground for speed control; 3- and 4-wire fans stutter
Current0.15 A (150 mA) or lessJ2 is limited to ~0.4 A including start-up
BearingBall or fluid / hydraulic (“FDB”)It will vibrate; plain sleeve bearings wear out
PlugAnyCut off: soldered to a JST SH plug (item 3) for J2
NoiseA 100 nF ceramic across the fan leads (item 5)No large capacitor: J2 switches the fan's ground
Reference partSunon MF30100V1-1000U-A99 (5.5 CFM, 0.12 A)Any brand matching the rows above
✗ Do not buy: 12 V or 24 V fans (those are phase 2, item 14) · 3- or 4-wire fans · Sleeve-bearing fans
✓ Check: Compare against your printed 30 × 30 × 10 mm reference block.

Starling 2 Max parts
Phase 2 — only if phase 1 fails. If the LED strip misbehaves on the drone's 3.3 V pad, it moves to its own 5 V buck module with a 74HCT125 on the peripheral power bus. If the fan causes trouble on J2, a 12 V or 24 V fan can run straight off the battery through the bus with a 0.2 A fuse.
