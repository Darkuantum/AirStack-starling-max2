1 · LED strip

addressable / NeoPixel type
The right type — flexible addressable strip
Count the wires: THREE
Photos: Cytron Technologies, product DS-6812-WS-601M. Example shown is 60 LED/m — exactly the density you want.
WS2812B  or  SK6812 5 V  ·  60 LED / metre  ·  1 metre  ·  must be individually addressable
Width: as narrow as possible — arm is ~10 mm, thinner at the motor. 5 mm “narrow” strip ideal, 10 mm bare strip is the maximum.
DO NOT BUYWhy
APA102 / DotStar / SK9822 / WS2801need a 4th clock wire — will not work
WS281512 V — wrong voltage
Non-addressable RGB stripwhole strip one colour only
Silicone-sleeved / waterproof tube strip (~14.5 mm)wider than the ~10 mm arm
Ask the shop: “Is this individually addressable, 5 volt, with 3 wires — data, +5 V, ground?” If it needs 4 wires, it is the wrong type.

2 · Cooling fan

size code “2507”
25 × 25 mm, 7 mm thick, exactly two wires

Drawn rather than photographed on purpose: the size and the wire count are what matter, and a photo of a different fan would mislead.

25 × 25 × 7 mm 5 V DC · 2 WIRES ONLY · ≤ 150 mA
Size code: “2507”

Shops know this size as 2507. Asking for “a 2507 5 volt 2-wire fan” is the fastest way to be understood.

DO NOT BUY ✗
12 V fanwrong voltage
3-wire (has tacho)will not work
4-wire (PWM input)will not work

Prefer a ball bearing fan over sleeve — it is going on a flying drone and will be vibrating.

30 mm or 20 mm is acceptable only if 25 mm is unavailable — ask me before buying a different size.

Why the wire count matters

The drone switches the fan's ground on and off to control speed. A 3- or 4-wire fan has extra pins that need a constant ground, so it will stutter or not start.

3 · Connector, resistors, wire

small parts
This shape — 2-pin housing with two wires already crimped on
Photo: Christopher.nunez, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons. Shown for shape only — confirm the pitch is 1.0 mm.
JST SH 1.0 mm 2-pin, female, with wires attached
(a “pigtail”)

This is the small JST — 1.0 mm between pins. Do not buy JST PH (2.0 mm) or XH (2.5 mm); they are bigger and will not fit.
Buy 2–3 spares, they are easy to damage.

330 Ω or 470 Ω 1/4 watt, ordinary carbon film
Buy 10 — they cost cents

Either value works. Anything between 300 and 500 Ω is fine.

Hook-up wire Thin stranded, 3 colours (red / black / any)
~2 m of each · 24–28 AWG

Optional — only if needed

5 V BEC / regulator module 5 V output, 1 A or more. Input must accept 7–17 V (it runs from a 4S battery).
Buy this only if the drone's LED power pad measures 3.3 V instead of 5 V. If unsure, skip it — it can be bought later.

4 · Propellers

7 inch tri-blade
Three blades, M5 centre bore
The bag to look for — label reads GEMFAN 7037-3
Photos: OddityRC / Gemfan 7037-3 product listing
7 × 3.7 × 3 7 inch diameter (178 mm)  ·  3.7 pitch  ·  3 blades  ·  M5 bore
Ask for “7037”. Buy 2 sets of 4 (each set is 2 CW + 2 CCW).
PITCH MUST NOT EXCEED 3.7. Do not accept 7040, 7042, 7045 or any “freestyle” propeller — a higher-pitch prop will overheat the motors and ESC on this aircraft. 7035 (3.5 pitch) is an acceptable substitute.

The same propeller fits both of our aircraft, even though their frames and motors differ.
